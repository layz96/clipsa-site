Privacy

Everything stays on the device

Analysis, matching and rendering all happen on your iPhone. Your videos, frames, analysis results and Photos identifiers are never sent to a server — there is no server. No account, no sign-up.

Straight talk

Worth knowing up front

The first launch takes a while

The app reads a few hundred of your most recent videos once — about a minute and a half. It keeps working through the rest in the background and pauses when the phone gets warm.

Videos in iCloud

Cloud videos take part in matching through their previews. For export the original is downloaded on a tap, with the remaining size shown.

Access to Photos is required

Without it there is nothing to build a reel from. Limited (“Selected Photos”) access works too — the app then uses only the videos you marked.

The interface is in Russian

Version 1.0 ships with a Russian interface; an English version is on the way. The support page walks through every screen in English.

Pricing

Clipsa is free right now

Version 1.0 comes with no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads. Paid plans, if they arrive, will come in a later update.
